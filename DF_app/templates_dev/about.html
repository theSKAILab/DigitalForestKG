What is INSPIRES?

INSPIRES stands for Intelligent Informatics and Smart Data for Improved Understanding of Northern Forest Ecosystem Resilience. The platform integrates tree inventory data, environmental climate normals, and species preference models into a unified knowledge graph, making complex forestry data accessible through both a graphical interface and a SPARQL query endpoint.

The Digital Forest platform supports decision-making for forest managers, enables peer-reviewed research on carbon capture and biodiversity, and provides an educational tool for students entering the field of forestry and environmental science.
