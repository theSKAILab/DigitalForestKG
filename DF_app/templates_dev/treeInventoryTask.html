How to use

  1. Select start and end years.
  2. Expand Angiosperms or Gymnosperms; drill down and click the bubble next to a taxon to select it.
  3. Click Load Filters.
  4. Tick filters you want, adjust ranges or options.
  5. Click Apply inventory filters.
